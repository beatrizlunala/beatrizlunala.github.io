Design Projects and Case Studies

An ongoing case study aiming to enhance the social experience at Farmers Horse Coffee, a cozy cafe local to Boston. How do we preserve the delicate balance between work-focused and social atmospheres in cafes? How can we encourage authentic interactions in an age where approaching strangers feels increasingly awkward?

We are constantly reading, and reading all different types of media. Note Mode is a case study I researched, designed, and prototyped based on the Learning Management System (LMS) Canvas, aiming to enhance the reading experience of college students.

Many readers struggle to maintain consistent reading habits and lack motivation to finish books. BookNook solves this by gamifying the reading experience with achievement-based goals, social features, and friendly competition that keeps users engaged.

An exploration of translating an analog interaction (making coffee with a coffee machine) to a digital interface. How does one replicate the user's experience of something physical within a digital space?

A collection of video call backgrounds to celebrate Employee Appreciation Week/Day.

A collection of miscellaneous graphics (printed and digital signage) completed during my co-op.

A set of informational posters on the typefaces Didot & Helvetica and their respective backgrounds.
