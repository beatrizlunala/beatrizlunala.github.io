The Latest

Current Project: Neighbor

I'm diving deep into an exciting interaction design project centered around Farmers Horse Coffee, a beloved local cafe near Northeastern's campus! Through extensive user research—including interviews with regulars and firsthand observations—I'm exploring how we might enhance the cafe experience and foster meaningful connections within intimate community spaces.

How do we preserve the delicate balance between work-focused and social atmospheres in cafes? How can we encourage authentic interactions in an age where approaching strangers feels increasingly awkward? What makes a space feel "lived-in" and inviting?

Take a look at my progress here and stay tuned for more updates as this project evolves!
