Bea Luna

I work to inspire thoughtfulness, creativity, and community!

I'm a Computer Science & Design student at Northeastern University with a passion for creating digital products that genuinely serve people's needs. With my technical background in front-end development and minor in Writing, in addition to my strong foundations in UX research and user-centered design principles, I'm drawn to exploring the intersections of art, technology, and design. I strive to create digital experiences that fulfill user needs with ease and clarity.

I'm also an active UX designer at Sandbox, where I'm wielding my skills to build a software online assessment program for a faculty client. Additionally, I continue to sharpen my technical writing skills in my work as a UX Writing Analyst at Northeastern's IT Services. You can also spot me at Digital Illustration Association meetings, where I previously served as a mentor to two art-enthusiast groups.

During my free time, I'm often seen powerwalking down Newbury Street, exploring new food places, or journaling at a local cafe with a shiny, new fountain pen I impulse purchased.
